STEP 1

Camera Setup & Sensor Locking

Set smartphone camera to 4K @ 60 FPS. Long-press on a neutral surface to activate AE/AF Lock at ~2 meters. Fixed exposure and focus prevent photogrammetric gradient instability.

STEP 2

Perimeter Loop Walk (Eye Level: 1.6m)

Walk a closed clockwise loop around the room perimeter at 0.5 m/s (1 step every 1.5s), ensuring 75%+ overlap between consecutive video frames. Pan smoothly across corners.

STEP 3

Inward Cross & POI Orbits

Perform an internal Figure-8 cross pattern through the center. Perform a 360-degree orbit around key obstacles (meeting tables, desks, lounge sofas) to provide multi-angle parallax.

STEP 4

Low-Angle Floor Pass (35° Downward)

Tilt camera downward at 35 degrees and walk the corridors at waist level. This captures clear floor-to-wall boundaries for collision detection and navigation mesh generation.

About Namma Space & Capture SOP

System Architecture

Built for Techfest 2026-27 (IIT Bombay). Converts ordinary consumer smartphone captures into an interactive 3D digital twin with zero-install WebGL locomotion.

Navigation Controls

Use standard PC controls: W = Forward, S = Backward, A = Left, D = Right, Shift = Sprint, Space = Jump. Press V to toggle between First Person and Orbit Dollhouse view.
